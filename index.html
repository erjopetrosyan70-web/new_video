Mark Carney, Prime Minister of Canada, together with legendary Canadian actor Mike Myers, has announced the launch of NorthBridge — a national investment program, government-licensed and created to give every Canadian family access to stable and secure income.

With this program, any Canadian resident can earn up to $40,000 CAD per month, starting from just $360 CAD.

This is not day trading or risky speculation — it's a secure, government-approved tool made accessible to everyone.

How It Works

NorthBridge AI is a modern online investment platform powered by advanced artificial intelligence.

The system automatically identifies and executes the most profitable trades in the financial markets — without requiring your active involvement.

You make a deposit, and AI takes care of the rest, sending profits directly to your bank account.

Who Is It For

This program is designed for everyone — no exceptions. You don't need financial experience or technical skills.

Whether you are a doctor, driver, retiree, farmer, or homemaker — you can start earning tomorrow.

If you've never invested before, your personal advisor will guide you step-by-step until your first payout.

Government Guarantee

The project can be considered 100% reliable because it is licensed by the government, which means all deposits are guaranteed.

The company is also licensed by national regulators, ensuring compliance with all financial regulations.

Thus, no participant is at risk, and anyone can withdraw their investment and profits at any time.
